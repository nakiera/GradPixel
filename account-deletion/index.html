Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This page explains how to request deletion of applicable data for SyMath, Fruit Pop and Mavi Ki Worlds, all developed by GradPixel.

Submit a Deletion Request

Email info@gradpixel.com with the game name, your request to delete your account or personal data, and enough information for us to identify the relevant records. If you are requesting deletion of support correspondence, mention that as well.

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment card details or other sensitive credentials. We may request reasonable ownership verification before deleting account-linked records. We review applicable requests and confirm the outcome by email.

SyMath

SyMath supports account deletion requests. Players can request removal of the applicable GradPixel player profile, account-linked game progress, cloud save information and related personal game data. An in-game Delete Account option may also be available, depending on the installed version.

Deletion and any subsequent new registration depend on successful ownership verification and completion of the applicable account-lifecycle process. A submitted request is not confirmation that deletion has finished. Deleted profile progress is not restored to a newly created profile.

Fruit Pop

Fruit Pop does not require a GradPixel player account. Its game progress, scores, items and preferences are primarily stored on the device. You may remove local game data through your device's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app, depending on your device; doing so can permanently remove progress or consumable items.

For deletion requests concerning support communications or other personal information held by GradPixel, email us using the instructions above. Data processed independently by advertising platforms and app stores may also be subject to those providers' own privacy and deletion procedures.

Mavi Ki Worlds

Mavi Ki Worlds is currently in development. Its existing browser prototypes do not require a GradPixel player account or maintain cloud-saved player profiles. Current prototype gameplay state is held in memory and resets when the page is refreshed or closed.

To request deletion of any personal data you have provided to GradPixel through support or testing communications, email us using the instructions above. Future Android or iOS builds may use advertising or other services; this page and the Mavi Ki Worlds Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the data actually processed before those features are released.

Deletion Scope and Retention

When a request is verified and applicable, we delete or de-identify personal records controlled by GradPixel that are covered by the request, including applicable player account information and associated game records. Local-only data must generally be cleared on the device by the player.

Limited records may need to be retained for legal compliance, accounting and purchase transactions, dispute handling, security or fraud prevention. Such records are kept only for the period required or permitted for the relevant purpose and applicable law; they are not used to reconstruct a deleted player profile. Where relevant, we will explain any retention or exceptions in our response to the request.

Google Play, Apple, advertising providers and other independent services control their own records and deletion processes. A request to GradPixel does not automatically delete records independently held by these providers.
