Fruit Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

GradPixel develops and publishes Fruit Pop. For privacy questions, contact info@gradpixel.com.

Accounts and Identity

Fruit Pop does not require a GradPixel player account or sign-in.

Game Data and Storage

Game progress, high scores, game items, mission progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. Local purchase-delivery records help prevent duplicate rewards.

Advertising, Purchases and Service Providers

The Android version uses Google Mobile Ads for advertising and Unity In-App Purchasing for purchases through Google Play. The iOS purchasing integration is under development. Advertising services may process device identifiers, technical information and advertising interaction data subject to applicable platform settings. Store providers process purchase transactions under their own privacy policies.

Relevant third-party policies: Google, Unity and Apple.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since Fruit Pop has no GradPixel player account, there is no GradPixel account to delete. Players can request assistance with applicable personal data through our support address. Removing locally stored game data may permanently erase progress and consumable items.

Limited records may be retained when necessary for applicable legal obligations, transaction records, security or fraud prevention. Retained information is not used to reconstruct a deleted game profile.

Security

We aim to limit data access to what is necessary for game operation, support and applicable legal obligations. Internet-based services involve security risks, and no transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.
