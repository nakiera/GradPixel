Mavi Ki Worlds Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

GradPixel develops Mavi Ki Worlds. For privacy inquiries, contact info@gradpixel.com.

Current Development and Testing Status

Mavi Ki Worlds is in early development. The available browser prototypes operate without a GradPixel player account, advertising SDK, purchase SDK, analytics SDK, or a game backend. They do not intentionally transmit gameplay progress to GradPixel servers.

This policy describes the current prototype, not a promise about features in future Android or iOS builds. We will update this policy before introducing account services, advertising, purchases, analytics, online saving, or other processing that changes how player data is handled.

Data and Local Storage

In the current browser prototypes, gameplay state is kept in the browser while the page is open. The prototype does not implement persistent account profiles or cloud saves. Your browser or the website hosting service may process technical connection information to deliver the page under their own applicable practices.

Accounts and Identity

The current prototypes do not require login or a GradPixel player account. Creating a Google Play Console application entry does not itself mean the prototype uses Google Play Games or collects player identity information.

Advertising and Purchases

No advertising or in-app purchasing integration is included in the current prototype. If these services are added to a released version, the privacy disclosures and relevant consent choices will be updated before their activation.

Children and Age-Appropriate Use

The game's intended age audience and any future child-directed features will be assessed before store release. This page does not assert that the game has completed age-rating or child-privacy review.

Retention and Deletion

The current prototypes do not create GradPixel player accounts or store account-linked gameplay progress on a GradPixel server. Closing or refreshing the prototype page resets in-memory game progress. For privacy inquiries or requests concerning any data provided to GradPixel through support communications, contact info@gradpixel.com. Applicable requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with relevant law.

Security

We aim to minimize collection and access to personal information. No internet-based transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We will revise this page if the game's data practices change and update the date shown above. Store disclosures will be aligned with the actual shipped build and included third-party SDKs.
